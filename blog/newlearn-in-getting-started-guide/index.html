Step 1

Learn to Navigate the Homepage

As soon as you open NewLearn.in, you'll see topic cards — AI Image Prompts, AI Video Prompts, Source Code, PDF/Image Tools, Books, and Build Websites. Each card takes you straight to its matching section, with no confusing menu structure to figure out.

Homepage topic cards — one click straight into a section

There are also "Topics" and "Blog" links in the top navigation bar, in case you want to browse a specific category directly.

Step 2

Find and Copy AI Prompts

01
Click "AI Image Prompts" or "AI Video Prompts"

You'll land straight in that section from the homepage.

02
Browse by category

Prompts are organized by style and use case, so you'll find what you need quickly.

03
Copy the prompt and paste it into your AI tool

Midjourney, Sora, Runway, Kling — whichever tool you're using, just paste it in.

💡
Beginner tip: Try the first 2–3 prompts unedited to see how a good prompt is structured. Then build your own variations from there.
Step 3

Download Source Code

Need ready-made code to build a website? Head to the "Source Code" section, pick the project type you need (landing page, portfolio, dashboard), and download the files.

📄 HTML/CSS/JS files 🔧 Copy, modify, deploy 🎓 Beginner-friendly structure
✅
Bonus: Check out the "How to Build Any Website without Coding Skills" series too — it also covers free hosting and a free domain for students.
Step 4

Access Free Books

The "Books & Downloads" section has curated AI and tech books, explained in plain language, each with a direct PDF download link. No sign-up wall — just download.

Step 5

Subscribe for Weekly Updates

There's an email subscribe box at the bottom of the homepage — every Tuesday, new prompts, tutorials, source code, and tool reviews land straight in your inbox. No spam, and you can unsubscribe any time.
