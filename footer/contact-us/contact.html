Writing to us about a problem?

A few details help us understand and fix the issue much faster:

  • The name of the tool and the page address (URL) you were using
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • Your device and browser, for example “Android, Chrome”
  • A screenshot, if you can share one (please do not send private documents)
